## What the plugin reads

`%LOCALAPPDATA%\LucasArts\Thrillville 07\Save` (the plugin reads your newest park save)

## What unlocks by itself

- The five story missions (Critics, Sabotage, Hypnosis, Robots, Traitor) unlock when the game saves after you finish them.
- Each park achievement (Explorer, Giant, Holiday, Otherworlds) unlocks once a save from that park has an open mission.
- Propaganda, The Rainbow and Disc Jockey unlock when you finish Propaganda Hunt!, Spray Away and Disc Collection.
- Coaster Boaster, Putt putt power and Whoa unlock from the matching building missions.
- Thrillville Talent, Sensation and Phenomenon unlock at park owner level 2, 5 and 10.

## Marked by hand

The other 32: minigame scores, party play, money and park life goals. Work on reading more of them is paused for now.
