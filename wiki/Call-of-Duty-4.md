# Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare

| Steam app ID | Achievements | Unlock automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7940 | 37 | 24 |

Full list with pictures: [Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare on the website](https://gnrraiser.github.io/Steam-Custom-Achievements/call-of-duty-4-modern-warfare/)

## What the plugin reads

Your single-player profile's `config.cfg`, in `players\profiles\<name>` inside the game folder (or under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\VirtualStore` or `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Activision\CoD4`)

## What unlocks by itself

Mission, Veteran and intel achievements unlock a few seconds after the game records campaign progress in your profile's config.cfg, which happens when a mission ends.

## Marked by hand

The 13 challenges inside missions (Down Boy Down, New Squadron Record, Rescue Roycewicz, Your Show Sucks, Man of the People, Straight Flush, Ghillies In The Mist, No Rest for the Weary, Daredevil, Roadkill, Bird on the Ground, Four of a Kind, Three of a Kind). The game doesn't save them.

## Tips

If you have several profiles, the plugin uses the one changed most recently. Pick a different one with **Settings > Use file / folder**.

If nothing unlocks, see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting).
