# Mass Effect

| Steam app ID | Achievements | Unlock automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 17460 | 50 (49 + PS3 platinum) | 45 |

Full list with pictures: [Mass Effect on the website](https://gnrraiser.github.io/Steam-Custom-Achievements/mass-effect/)

## What the plugin reads

`Documents\BioWare\Mass Effect\Save\Profile.MassEffectProfile` (shared by all your characters)

## What unlocks by itself

Mass Effect on PC keeps the Xbox 360 achievements in its own profile file. The plugin reads it, so 45 achievements unlock a few seconds after the game records them.

## Marked by hand

Scholar, Completionist, Tactician and Medal of Exploration, until their places in the profile are confirmed. A profile that already has any of them unlocked would settle it (see the [Roadmap](Roadmap)).

If nothing unlocks, see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting).
