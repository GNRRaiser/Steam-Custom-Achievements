# Assassin's Creed Brotherhood

| Steam app ID | Achievements | Unlock automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 48190 | 61 (60 + PS3 platinum) | 50 |

Full list with pictures: [Assassin's Creed Brotherhood on the website](https://gnrraiser.github.io/Steam-Custom-Achievements/assassins-creed-brotherhood/)

## What the plugin reads

`%USERPROFILE%\Saved Games\Assassin's Creed Brotherhood\SAVES`

## What unlocks by itself

Brotherhood records its own achievements (Settings > Stats > Achievements in the game) in the OPTIONS file next to your save. The plugin reads that record and your newest save, so the 50 single-player and Da Vinci Disappearance achievements unlock a few seconds after the game unlocks them.

Capture the Flag counts Borgia flags out of 101 and In Memoriam counts feathers out of 100, from the list of every flag and feather you have taken.

## Marked by hand

The 10 multiplayer achievements (Synchronization Established, Needle in a Haystack, Strong Closer, Fast Learner, Job Skills, Download Complete, Role Model, Overachiever, Abstergo Employee of the Month, Ahead of the Curve), since the multiplayer servers are shut down.

If nothing unlocks, see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting).
