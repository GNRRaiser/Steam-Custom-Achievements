# Call of Duty: World at War

| Steam app ID | Achievements | Unlock automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10090 | 67 (66 + PS3 platinum) | 22 |

Full list with pictures: [Call of Duty: World at War on the website](https://gnrraiser.github.io/Steam-Custom-Achievements/call-of-duty-world-at-war/)

## What the plugin reads

Your single-player profile's `config.cfg`, in `players\profiles\<name>` inside the game folder (or under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\VirtualStore` or `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Activision\CoDWaW`)

## What unlocks by itself

Mission, Veteran and Death Card achievements unlock a few seconds after the game records campaign progress in your profile's config.cfg, which happens when a mission ends.

## Marked by hand

19 challenges inside missions, 5 co-op and multiplayer achievements, and the 20 Map Pack 2 and Map Pack 3 achievements. None of these are saved in a file the plugin can read.

## Tips

A few Map Pack 3 achievements don't have an unlock rate yet.

If nothing unlocks, see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting).
