# Bully: Scholarship Edition

| Steam app ID | Achievements | Unlock automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12200 | 56 | 21 |

Full list with pictures: [Bully: Scholarship Edition on the website](https://gnrraiser.github.io/Steam-Custom-Achievements/bully-scholarship-edition/)

The list is the 38 Xbox 360 achievements, the 5 achievements only the PS4 version had, the Valedictorian platinum, and the 12 multiplayer achievements of the mobile Anniversary Edition.

## What the plugin reads

`Documents\Bully Scholarship Edition` (the plugin reads your newest `BullyFile1`-`BullyFile6` save and the load screen's `FileTableBully`)

## What unlocks by itself

- Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior and Graduate unlock from the chapter your save is in.
- Perfectionist unlocks at 100% completion, as shown on the load screen.
- Counter achievements unlock from the game's own Stats screen numbers and show n / total: Marathon, Skate Pro, Tour de Bullworth (distances), Eggsellent! (cars egged), Skidmark (wedgies), Kickin' the Balls (soccer balls kicked), The Wheel Deal (wheelies), Smell Ya Later (stink bomb hits), Watch Your Step (people tripped with marbles), Soda-licious (sodas bought), Smart Mouth (taunts), Down for the Count (people knocked out), Glass Dismissed (shooting gallery bottles), and the PS4 Kleptomania (room trophies) and The (Expert) Bicycle Thief (bikes jacked).
- Valedictorian unlocks once every Xbox 360 and PS4 achievement is unlocked. It doesn't wait for the Anniversary Edition ones.

## Marked by hand

Missions, classes, errands, races, lawn mowing, the paper route, kisses, the carnival and arcade games, Green Thumbs Up, Sharp Dressed Man, Rich Kid Blues, Popularity Contest and Problem Child. The 12 Anniversary Edition achievements are for the mobile version's multiplayer, so they can only be marked by hand on PC.

If nothing unlocks, see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting).
