# Big Picture mode

The plugin works in Big Picture mode as well as desktop Steam.

## Where to find it

Open a supported game and go to its **Your Stuff** tab. The **Achievements** section sits above Media and is laid out like Steam's own: your unlocked count and bar, your newest unlock featured with the other unlocks beside it, and a row of small locked pictures.

Opening it shows a full-screen achievements page with the game's cover art, title, earned bar, play time, **My Achievements** and **Settings** tabs, and the list. The header shrinks to one line as you scroll and grows back at the top.

## Controller

| Where | Button | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your Stuff tab | D-pad down from the tabs (or up from the section below) | Moves into the Achievements section |
| Achievements section | Left / Right | Picks an achievement |
| Achievements section | A | Opens the full-screen page |
| Full-screen page | D-pad | Moves through the list |
| Full-screen page | A | Marks a manual achievement as unlocked or locked |
| Full-screen page | LB / RB | Switches between My Achievements and Settings |
| Full-screen page | B (or Esc, or the BACK button) | Goes back |

Popups appear in Big Picture as well.

## If something's off

Open the full-screen page's **Settings** tab and use **Copy status**. It includes a "controller" entry per Big Picture window (button presses seen, the last one, and where Steam's focus landed), which is the first thing to look at if a button doesn't respond. Include it when you report a problem.
