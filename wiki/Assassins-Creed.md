# Assassin's Creed

| Steam app ID | Achievements | Unlock automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15100 | 44 | 44 |

Full list with pictures: [Assassin's Creed on the website](https://gnrraiser.github.io/Steam-Custom-Achievements/assassins-creed/)

## What the plugin reads

`%APPDATA%\Ubisoft\Assassin's Creed\Saved Games`

## What unlocks by itself

Assassin's Creed keeps its own record of every achievement in its save, and the plugin reads it, so all 44 unlock by themselves a few seconds after the game saves following the moment the game awards them.

The story objectives and the save's own lists back this up and show progress as you play: Personal Vendetta (60 Templars), every flag Keeper, Fearless (91 viewpoints), and the running counts for Eagle's Prey, Swiftness, Talon, Dance and March of the Pious.

## Marked by hand

None. Every achievement is automatic.

If nothing unlocks, see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting).
