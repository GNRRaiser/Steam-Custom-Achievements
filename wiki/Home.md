# Steam Custom Achievements

Steam Custom Achievements is a free [Millennium](https://steambrew.app) plugin that adds Xbox 360 achievements to PC games whose Steam version has none. Each supported game gets an **ACHIEVEMENTS** card on its Steam Library page, unlock popups and a full "View My Achievements" list, in desktop Steam and in Big Picture mode.

Most achievements unlock by themselves: the plugin reads the game's saves (read-only) and unlocks an achievement a few seconds after the game records it, including ones you earned before you installed the plugin. The rest have a **Mark as unlocked** button.

It's an unofficial fan plugin. The achievements show only in your own Steam client, not on your Steam profile.
