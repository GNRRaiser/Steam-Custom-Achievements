Full list with pictures: [Fallout 3 Game of the Year Edition on the website](https://gnrraiser.github.io/Steam-Custom-Achievements/fallout-3/)

## What the plugin reads

`Documents\My Games\Fallout3\Saves` (or the same folder under OneDrive)

## What unlocks by itself

Fallout 3 lost its Games for Windows Live achievements in a 2021 Steam update, so these are the Xbox 360 list for the base game and all five DLCs. The plugin reads your newest save: finished quests, the Pip-Boy statistics (people and creatures killed, locks, terminals, speech, locations, bobbleheads), and your level and karma.

Like the game, the level-and-karma achievements (Reaver, Mercenary, Protector and the rest) unlock when you level up, for the karma you have at that moment. Changing your karma alone waits for your next level-up.

## Marked by hand

None. Every achievement is automatic.

If nothing unlocks, see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting).
