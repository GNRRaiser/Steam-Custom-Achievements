# Mass Effect 2

| Steam app ID | Achievements | Unlock automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 24980 | 63 (62 + PS3 platinum) | 61 |

Full list with pictures: [Mass Effect 2 on the website](https://gnrraiser.github.io/Steam-Custom-Achievements/mass-effect-2/)

## What the plugin reads

`Documents\BioWare\Mass Effect 2\BIOGame\Profile\Player1.prf` (shared by all your characters)

## What unlocks by itself

Mass Effect 2 on PC records its achievements in its own profile, the same list it shows under Extras. The plugin reads it, so 61 achievements, DLC included, unlock a few seconds after the game records them.

## Marked by hand

Highly Trained. It's an Xbox 360 achievement the PC game doesn't have.
