Assassin's Creed II Steam achievements

Assassin's Creed II has no achievements on Steam, but the Xbox 360 version has 50, plus a PS3 platinum trophy. Steam Custom Achievements is a free plugin that adds all 51 of them to Assassin's Creed II in your Steam Library (Steam app 33230). 41 unlock automatically and 10 have a "Mark as unlocked" button.

Download from GitHubSteam install guide

How Assassin's Creed II achievements unlock

Story, tomb and supported activity achievements unlock a few seconds after Assassin's Creed II autosaves, and so do Art Connoisseur, Perfect Harmony, Show Your Colors and Handy Man, read from the game's own checklists and villa levels in the save. The rest are marked by hand from the achievement list.

Install

  1. Install Millennium 3.x for Steam on Windows.
  2. Download the latest steam-custom-achievements-vX.Y.Z-share.zip from GitHub Releases.
  3. Exit Steam completely (Steam > Exit), extract the ZIP and run INSTALL_OR_REPAIR.cmd.
  4. Start Steam, check that Steam Custom Achievements is on in Millennium > Plugins, and open a supported game in your Library.

The step by step Steam guide has pictures and fixes for common problems.
