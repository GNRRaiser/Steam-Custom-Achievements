Call of Duty: World at War Steam achievements

Call of Duty: World at War has no achievements on Steam, but the Xbox 360 version has 66, plus a PS3 platinum trophy. Steam Custom Achievements is a free plugin that adds all 67 of them to Call of Duty: World at War in your Steam Library (Steam app 10090). 22 unlock automatically and 45 have a "Mark as unlocked" button.

Download from GitHubSteam install guide

How Call of Duty: World at War achievements unlock

Mission, Veteran and Death Card achievements unlock a few seconds after the game records campaign progress in your profile's config.cfg (when a mission ends). Feats inside missions, co-op, multiplayer and Nazi Zombies are marked by hand.

Install

  1. Install Millennium 3.x for Steam on Windows.
  2. Download the latest steam-custom-achievements-vX.Y.Z-share.zip from GitHub Releases.
  3. Exit Steam completely (Steam > Exit), extract the ZIP and run INSTALL_OR_REPAIR.cmd.
  4. Start Steam, check that Steam Custom Achievements (Local) is on in Millennium > Plugins, and open a supported game in your Library.

The step by step Steam guide has pictures and fixes for common problems.
